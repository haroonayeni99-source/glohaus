# GLOHAUS logical feature consolidation — review addendum

5 November 2026. Part of the existing visual consistency brief. Recommendation-only; no production or structural application changes.

## Binding design clarification

GLOHAUS must retain the rich, premium visual identity of Marketplace/Shop and Discover: attractive imagery, berry/pink gradients, rounded components, layered cards, expressive typography and useful content density. Simplicity means clearer organisation and fewer redundant steps. It does not mean minimalism, empty screens, removing imagery or shrinking the feature set.

The Direction 01 prototype is a component/spacing exploration, not approval to replace the rich production homepage, desktop rails, Marketplace or Discover with a reduced layout. Preserve those strengths while consolidating navigation and genuinely overlapping functions. Haroon's positive initial feedback is not approval to deploy major structural changes.

## Evidence basis

Audited the 81-page source inventory, account navigation, customer/professional appointment pages and shared BookingNavigation, Business tools index, customer invitation page, professional referral redirect, customer/professional notification settings, notification inbox, marketing preferences, Shop cart entry and Marketplace navigation. Recommendations below are source-grounded; authenticated task walkthroughs, route-link audits and physical-device/PWA acceptance remain release gates.

Together/gifting/group contributions were not found as a customer-facing implemented workflow in this baseline. Marketplace gift sets are a product collection; Admin recognition/gifts are internal records. Neither proves service gifting or pooled booking payments already exist. Do not rebrand those unrelated functions as a completed Together feature.

## Route and navigation safety contract

- Grouping can ship using existing URLs and shared subnavigation; a new parent page does not require migrating records.
- For any truly retired duplicate route, propose a route-by-route mapping before approval. Preserve query strings, fragments where relevant, IDs and sign-in return destinations. Do not blanket-redirect entire route families.
- Keep existing deep links to booking details, order details, policies, notification settings, referral codes and OAuth callbacks.
- Use task links/anchors as navigation; do not use ARIA tab roles unless the keyboard behaviour and panel semantics are implemented correctly.
- Never show blank/default “all accounts” data during a role switch. Confirm role and account context server-side before fetching protected content.
- Retain a direct way to reach every function when bottom navigation is absent, at tablet breakpoints, in landscape, in an installed PWA and with the on-screen keyboard open.

6. Validate affected routes across desktop/tablet/mobile, light/night/system, keyboard/reduced-motion, iPhone Safari, Android Chrome and installed PWAs. Real-device acceptance remains outstanding.
