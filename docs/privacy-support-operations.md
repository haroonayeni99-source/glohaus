# Privacy and support operations

Owner → Support & Disputes reuses existing tickets, case history, appeals, evidence and email alerts. An explicit “Open review” loads the bounded Owner/MFA-only privacy queue; no new dashboard polling is added. A selected case records its purpose, original receipt date, proportionate identity review, justified retention hold, review date and reason. Metadata changes create private history and immutable audit entries. Existing guest access never exposes identity/hold metadata. Ordinary Admins cannot make these decisions or resolve privacy cases. A data-rights case needs recorded identity review and a requester-visible outcome before closure.

## Operating procedure

1. Log requests received by email, verbally or through another channel in the existing support system. Use the original receipt date, not the later ticket-entry date. The Owner can correct it to an earlier date with an audit reason. A ticket link or email sender alone does not prove identity.
2. Acknowledge promptly using the existing two-weekday initial-response target. Review identity and representative authority proportionately; prefer existing account/booking checks. Do not collect unnecessary identity documents or put full exports into notes.
3. Normally respond to rights requests without undue delay within one calendar month. The dashboard uses a conservative corresponding-calendar-date target in Europe/London, including month ends/leap years. “Awaiting customer” does not automatically pause it. Document any legally justified pause or complex/numerous-request extension with dates, grounds and timely notice; the displayed target is not automatically extended.
4. Acknowledge privacy complaints within 30 days, investigate without undue delay, provide updates and a reasoned outcome. Ticket appeals and escalations retain their existing Owner decision route. Explain the person's ICO complaint option. General complaints still use existing case targets and evidence-based handling.
5. Search relevant records, protect third-party information and supply the response using an appropriate secure delivery channel. Record what was supplied, corrected, restricted, erased or lawfully retained. Do not treat a case note as proof that the underlying action occurred.
6. Review retention monthly and the policy annually. Review support evidence 90 days after closure and case messages/decisions 24 months after final closure. Review inactive accounts and identifiable acquisition/referral evidence after 24 months; earlier eligible requests and existing shorter journey/location limits still apply. Keep minimal accounting/tax records for six years after the relevant financial/tax year ends, longer where required; confirm annual obligations with the accountant. Honour marketing opt-outs immediately and review minimal consent/suppression evidence annually.
7. A hold needs a specific purpose and a dated recheck (within the next year). The queue includes overdue open-case holds and closed-case reviews, including cases outside the latest 100 inbox rows. Remove/anonymise unneeded data through the authorised existing account/provider processes, document completion, recipients and backup/provider follow-up. This release adds **no automatic deletion or financial-record erasure**. A justified hold does not permit indefinite retention without review.
