# GLOHAUS recruitment copy

## Website introduction

We’re building GLOHAUS to support independent beauty professionals: a place to showcase your work, manage bookings and help new customers discover your business. Keep your regular clients and existing booking methods, and choose how GLOHAUS fits around you. Our aim is to make beauty bookings clearer, fairer and easier for customers and professionals.

## Initial outreach

Hi [name], we came across your [specific genuine work] and would love to introduce GLOHAUS. We’re building a beauty marketplace where independent professionals can showcase their work, manage bookings and grow their visibility while keeping the flexibility of their own business. We’re inviting professionals to help shape the early platform through our Founding Professionals Programme. Would you be open to taking a look?

## When they are interested

Our Founding Professionals Programme includes an initial 14 days at 0% GLOHAUS commission. Eligible participants who complete the agreed feedback questionnaire can receive an additional 30 days at 0% GLOHAUS commission. Honest feedback is welcome, whether positive or critical. Applicable payment-processing and customer booking fees are separate, and we’ll show you the relevant fees and terms before you commit. This is a temporary launch programme, subject to its current availability and your confirmed eligibility.

## Beta participation

During this beta, we’re asking participating professionals to try GLOHAUS with their own customers so we can learn from real appointments. Please share your booking link with your customers only for now, rather than promoting it publicly. You can continue handling regular clients through your usual channels and choose which appointments to bring to GLOHAUS. We cannot promise a particular number of new customers or earnings.

## Tools follow-up

Your GLOHAUS profile can bring your services, bookings, reviews and portfolio together. You can add Instagram post/Reel and TikTok video links to showcase work you already share. Calendar and social account connections will be available only where the provider setup, permissions and testing have been completed; we’ll explain what is available for your account before you connect anything.

Do not claim a large team, established customer base, guaranteed growth, instant calendar sync or approval of an external integration that has not been granted. Keep current fees, programme eligibility and full terms accessible before acceptance.
