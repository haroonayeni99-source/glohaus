# Professional growth release 1

Reuse the existing professional performance page as a Business Hub, preserving its layout classes, acquisition report, invitation/QR and referral controls. Add personal booking-page and existing management links, clearly separated captured-payment/service-value labels, returning-client history, within-period multiple-visit counts, confirmed future booking counts, and mature 30/60/90-day return cohorts. The cohort's first-visit window ends one observation window before today, so recent clients are never counted as failures to return. An empty cohort shows no estimated percentage.

All business metrics exclude cancelled, unpaid, fully refunded, future-completed and disputed appointments. Partial refunds contribute only their actual net captured value. Business reporting is not referral reward qualification; no reward hold or financial rule changes. Reuse the existing professional-scoped dispute-summary function rather than granting browser access to dispute tables. The personal page is shared only for a published profile.

Local verification: 37 database/dashboard/platform tests passed, including returning clients outside the selected period, cohort maturity/boundaries, longer service cycles, deposit/refund accounting, dispute exclusion, future bookings and cross-professional RLS. TypeScript/lint/build are recorded in the PR as completed. Production deployment requires passing CI and staged query verification; no migration is required. Authenticated browser/physical-device checks must be reported separately. Client imports, academy cohorts, automatic rebooking, bundles, subscription and AI are separate releases.
