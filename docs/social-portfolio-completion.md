### Bounded large-account imports

A final reliability review found that the previous ten-page guard rejected an entire social sync whenever more history existed. The follow-up imports a conservative recent window (at most 1,000 Instagram posts or 200 TikTok videos) and bounds all social pagination to 25 seconds, retaining the existing eight-second per-request timeout. Imported items outside the current returned window stop displaying; this also avoids retaining old public content whose availability could no longer be checked within the bounded scan. Featured imported work is subject to this window. Manual post links remain independent. The dashboard explains the cutoff. Provider/network errors still preserve the last successful snapshot rather than committing an incomplete failed request. Real large-account/API acceptance remains gated by approved credentials.
